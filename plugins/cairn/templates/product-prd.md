# Product PRD: <product>

> Lives on main at `docs/sdlc/product/PRD.md`. Every feature plan inherits this.

## Vision
What the product is and the change it makes for its users. Three sentences.

## Users
Each user type: who they are, the job they hire the product for, what success looks like.

- <user type>: <job, success>

## Non-goals
What the product will not become, each with a reason.

- <thing> because <reason>

## Non-functional requirements
Product-wide constraints with numbers: performance, availability, security, accessibility, privacy.

- **NFR-01** <measurable constraint>

## Must not trade off
The few properties that outrank any feature or deadline.

- <property>: <why it is sacred>

## Roadmap
One line per feature plan, highest priority first. Status is generated from plan state.

- [P0] <roadmap item>: <one-line outcome>
- [P1] <roadmap item>: <one-line outcome>
