The interview never asked what a build was for, review covered only risky tasks, and security appeared only at review. Discover is now goal-first: it opens with a guess and a confidence number, asks one question at a time, and ends with a read-back card that needs an explicit yes. Every task gets a five-axis review, and risky ones also go to the new read-only `security-reviewer`; the PRD gets a threat model, and check scans for secrets and audits dependencies. `cairn close` no longer waits for the merge, and status shows "Closed · PR open" until the branch is in main. New review and security checklists were added.
