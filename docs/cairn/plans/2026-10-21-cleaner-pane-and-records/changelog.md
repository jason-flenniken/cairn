The pane became easier to read and records easier to find. Before, cards changed height on every tool call, a follow-up count nobody could act on kept growing, and plan folders did not sort by date. The pane now has three tabs: Now, History (the full timeline) and Records (every plan, with the record opening in the pane). New plans live in `docs/cairn/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>/`, and commands find old and new names. A plan closes with at most five open follow-ups, merged at close and expiring after two declines or 60 days. `cairn close` now requires a Findings section in the record. Added the `cairn records` command.
