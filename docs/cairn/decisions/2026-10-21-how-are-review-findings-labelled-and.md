# How are review findings labelled and handled?

**Decision:** Critical and Required are fixed before merge, Optional and Nit become follow-ups; every task gets a five-axis review and risky tasks also get the security reviewer.

**Why:** Real findings are cheapest to fix while the task is fresh.
