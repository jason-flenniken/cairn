# How are follow-ups and findings kept bounded?

**Decision:** At most 5 open follow-ups per plan, expiring after 2 declines or 60 days; the recorder writes the record's Findings from the plan log, learn asks nothing, and close requires Findings.

**Why:** One plan's 17 follow-ups buried anything useful.
