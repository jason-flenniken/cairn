# How is the pane laid out?

**Decision:** Three tabs (Now, History, Records) and a bottom-pinned bar; Now shows fixed-height clickable cards with the main session first; the pane polls state every 2 seconds and works inline at 48 columns.

**Why:** One place to watch a plan, with cards that don't jump.
