# How are plan folders named and found?

**Decision:** Plan folders are <local-date>-<slug> under docs/cairn/plans; lookup prefers the dated folder, falls back to the undated one, and resolves through the main checkout.

**Why:** Folders sort by date, and old branches keep finding their records.
